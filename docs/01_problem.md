# 1. Problem

- FPV drones are cheap, fast and precise. They are used massively against vehicles and people.
- Unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) are increasingly used to evacuate wounded people from dangerous areas, so that fewer rescuers are exposed.
- These robots move slowly, are only lightly protected and have almost no means to detect an incoming drone in time.
- The last seconds before an attack are decisive. Without early detection, no protective measure can react.

**Goal:** give a ground robot "eyes" for small drones in its near zone — reliably, quickly and at a price that allows mass deployment.
