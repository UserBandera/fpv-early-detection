# FPV Early Detection — sensor module for ground robots

**Early detection of small FPV drones to protect unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) that evacuate wounded people.**
Defense / dual-use technology, developed first of all for Ukraine.

> Status: concept & first prototype in preparation (TRL 2–3) · Location: Berlin–Brandenburg, Germany · Started: September 2026

## The problem
Small FPV drones have become one of the main threats on the battlefield. Robotic ground vehicles used to evacuate wounded people are slow, lightly protected and easy targets. Their crews — and the wounded on board — need warning and protection in the last seconds before an attack.

## Our approach (public overview)
A compact, low-cost **sensor module** that can be mounted on different ground robots:
- **Radar** — detects and measures moving objects in the near zone
- **Camera** — provides images for recognition
- **AI on an embedded computer** — distinguishes drones from other objects
- **Output** — an alert and target track for the robot's protective systems

Design goals: low cost per unit, robustness (dust, fog, vibration), and scalability to large quantities.
